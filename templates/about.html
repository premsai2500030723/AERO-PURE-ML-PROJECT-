Air Quality Analysis & Prediction

This project performs comprehensive exploratory data analysis and machine learning modelling on hourly air quality measurements from the Aotizhongxin monitoring station in Beijing. The goal is to understand pollution patterns and build predictive models for PM2.5 concentration.
